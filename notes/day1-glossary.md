Embedding: A string of numbers that translates text into machine-readable math so computers can instantly recognize when two different phrases share the same underlying meaning.

Vector Store: A specialized search engine built specifically to hold these numerical text codes and rapidly pull up the most relevant matches based on concept rather than exact words.

Chunking: The act of chopping up lengthy articles or documents into smaller snippets so the AI isn't forced to digest too much unnecessary text at one time.

Context Window: The ultimate text capacity limit that a large language model can actually hold in its active memory during a single conversational turn.

Hallucination: A glitch where an AI confidently invents completely fictitious facts or details when it lacks the actual information needed to give a real answer.
